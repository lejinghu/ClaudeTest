    How to play

    You are the Defender. The AI is the Attacker. Each round you take 3 actions, then the Attacker takes 3.

    • You win at the end of your turn with a Segmentation Score of 10, or if round 12 ends.
    • The Attacker wins by exfiltrating 2 Crown Jewels.

    Your actions: DFW 1-2-3-4, plus SSP

    1. Assess: +2 Insight. Insight pays for everything else. Stage 1: Segmentation Assessment.
    2. Harden an infrastructure service (NTP, DNS, LDAP) for 1 Insight. It evicts any stone there, closes the hub shortcut, and is no longer an exit. +1 score. Stage 2: Infrastructure Services.
    3. Segment: place up to 2 walls on border edges (between apps, infra or zones) for 1 Insight. Seal every Dev/Prod boundary edge for +2 score and leakage alerts. Stage 3: Zones.
    4. Ring-fence an app for Insight equal to its size. Its whole border is blocked, except business flows. +2 score. After that you can also wall edges inside it. Stage 4: App microsegmentation.
    5. ★ Deploy a face-down Sensor or Decoy on an empty cell for 1 Insight. SSP: IDS/IPS on allowed traffic.

    The Attacker

    • Breach (once per turn): place a stone on row 1 or the internet-facing storefront H.
    • Spread: place a stone next to one of theirs across an open edge. Business flows are always open. Unhardened NTP, DNS and LDAP all connect to each other (the hub).
    • Recon: secretly look at a face-down token next to one of their stones.
    • Exfil: take a revealed Jewel whose stone is connected to an exit: row 1, storefront H, or unhardened infra.

    Tokens and capture

    • A stone that enters a Jewel reveals it. A Sensor removes the stone and ends the Attacker’s turn. A Decoy does nothing.
    • Quarantine: an attacker group with no open edge to an empty cell is removed. +1 score.

    Shortcuts: 1–5 pick an action · Esc cancel · U undo · E end turn.
