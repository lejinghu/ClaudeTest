Hide 3 Crown Jewels and 3 Sensors, each in a different app. The Attacker can see where your tokens are, but not what they are.

Tap a cell to place a token (Jewels first). Tap a token to turn a Jewel into a Sensor, or to remove a Sensor. Tap another cell in the same app to move its token there. Careful: a Jewel on row 1 or in the storefront H sits on an exit.

    How to play

    You are the Defender. The AI is the Attacker. Each round you take 3 actions, then the Attacker takes 3.

    • You win at the end of your turn with a Segmentation Score of 7, or if round 8 ends.
    • The Attacker wins by exfiltrating one Crown Jewel.

    A game takes about 5 minutes.

    Business flows are hidden

    Each game, apps talk to each other over hidden business flows. Security Intelligence reveals them as traffic history builds up: everyday flows from round 2, month-end flows in round 3. Month-end flows first run in round 4.

    If your policy blocks a real flow while it runs, that’s an outage: −2 score, and an emergency allow rule opens the edge again, for the Attacker too.

    Your actions: DFW 1-2-3-4, plus SSP

    • 1 Assess: +2 Insight. Insight pays for everything else. Stage 1: Segmentation Assessment.
    • 2 Harden an infrastructure service (NTP, DNS, LDAP) for 1 Insight. It evicts any stone there, closes the hub shortcut, and is no longer an exit. +1 score. Stage 2: Infrastructure Services.
    • 3 Segment: place any number of walls on border edges (between apps, or next to infra) in one action, for 1 Insight per 2 walls. You have 12 walls in all. Good for cutting an attacker chain.
    • 4 Ring-fence an app for Insight equal to its size. Security Intelligence’s recommendation is published with it: every observed flow on the app’s border gets an allow rule, and everything else is blocked. +1 score. Afterwards you can also wall edges inside it. Stage 4: App microsegmentation.
    • + Allow: add any number of exceptions in one action, for flows found after the lockdown (free) or manual guesses (1 Insight each).
    • ★ Deploy a face-down Sensor on an empty cell for 1 Insight. SSP: IDS/IPS inspects the traffic you allow.
    • ⇄ Swap two face-down tokens, or only pretend to. The Attacker can’t tell, so everything it scouted on those two becomes a guess. It’s costly (3 Insight, max 2 per game, or −1 score with the other rule), once per turn, and not next to an attacker stone.

    The real workflow: observe traffic → publish exceptions for valid flows → lock down everything else. Lock down before Security Intelligence has seen the traffic and you break production.

    The Attacker

    • Breach (once per turn): place a stone on row 1 or the internet-facing storefront H.
    • Spread: place a stone next to one of theirs across an open edge. Unhardened NTP, DNS and LDAP all connect to each other (the hub). Getting through a ring-fence over an allowed flow means exploiting that service, which costs 2 actions. That’s why Sensors (IDS/IPS) on allowed flows matter.
    • Recon: secretly look at a face-down token next to one of their stones.
    • Exfil: take a revealed Jewel whose stone is connected to an exit: row 1, storefront H, or unhardened infra.

    Tokens and capture

    • A stone that enters a Jewel reveals it. A Sensor removes the stone and ends the Attacker’s turn. The red % under each token is the Attacker’s current jewel odds for it.
    • Quarantine: an attacker group with no open edge to an empty cell is removed. +1 score.

    Shortcuts: 1–7 pick an action · Esc cancel · U undo · E end turn.
