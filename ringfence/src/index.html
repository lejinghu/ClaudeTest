    How to play

    You are the Defender of a datacenter. The AI is the Attacker. Each round you take 3 actions, then it takes 3.

    • You win by reaching the Zero Trust target (18 points on Normal), or by surviving to the end of round 8.
    • The Attacker wins by stealing one Crown Jewel, or by getting footholds in 7 apps: ransomware across a huge blast radius.

    How you score: territory

    At the start of each of your turns, every secure app (ring-fenced, with no attacker inside) and every hardened service gives +1 Zero Trust point. You also get +3 Insight, plus 1 per secure app. Secure early and your economy grows.

    Your actions

    • 👁 Observe an app (free, one action). Security Intelligence maps its business flows, and they show on your next turn. Stage 1: assess before you enforce.
    • 🛡 Harden a shared service (2 Insight). The dots on each app show which of NTP, DNS and LDAP it uses. An unhardened service is a backdoor into every app that uses it, even ring-fenced ones, and DNS is also an exit (tunnelling). Stage 2: infrastructure services.
    • ◎ Ring-fence an app (3 Insight). The observed flows get allow rules and everything else is blocked. Fencing an app you haven’t observed breaks its flows: an outage, −2 points. Stage 4: microsegmentation.
    • ★ Sensor (1 Insight): a face-down IDS/IPS trap. A stone that steps on it is removed, and the Attacker’s turn ends. SSP threat prevention.
    • ⛔ Isolate (2 Insight): an emergency block on any edge, even a business flow. Breaking a running flow costs −2 points. Incident response.

    The Attacker

    • Breach (once per turn) into row 1 or the storefront H. Spread to a neighbouring cell. Moving into a different app, through an allowed flow, or through a service backdoor costs extra actions.
    • Recon peeks at a face-down token. Exfil steals a revealed jewel connected to an exit, but not on the turn it was found, so you always get a turn to respond.

    Big decisions: which service to harden first (whose apps does it expose?), which apps to observe and secure (income or jewels?), and when to spend on Sensors or Isolate. Shortcuts: 1 Observe · 2 Harden · 3 Ring-fence · 4 Sensor · 5 Isolate · U undo · E end turn.
